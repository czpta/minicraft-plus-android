# Font credits

`font_monocraft.png` is a bitmap sheet rendered from **Monocraft** by Idrees Hassan
(https://github.com/IdreesInc/Monocraft), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 - see `Monocraft-OFL.txt`.
It is generated by `tools/make_font_sheet.py` from `tools/Monocraft.ttf`; glyphs the font lacks or that don't fit the
game's 8x8 cell keep the game's original glyphs. `font_classic.png` is the game's own font sheet, unchanged.
